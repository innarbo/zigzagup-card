Beyond IQ

Building judgment in the age of answers.

AI can accelerate access to answers. ZigZagUp develops the human capacity to evaluate them - by making reasoning visible, diagnosable, repairable, and transferable.

A diagnosis-first reasoning calibration and training platform for high-stakes learning.

01

Why now

AI can generate answers in seconds. The scarce skill is judgment: verifying reasoning, evidence, assumptions, and confidence before accepting an answer.

02

Human potential

Human potential is not only what you know or how fast you think. It is how effectively you monitor, challenge, and redirect your own thinking.

03

What's missing

Personalized learning still misses the why. Most systems adapt content or generate explanations. ZigZagUp diagnoses where thinking broke - and what repair is needed.

04

What ZigZagUp does

Not another answer engine. A diagnostic repair loop: predict before choices, expose the reasoning path, repair the breakpoint, and test whether the repair transfers.
